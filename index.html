Selected Works

Where the Wild Things Are

2019 — ongoing

A wandering study of children and landscape, shot at the edge of dusk. The series returns, year after year, to the same unanswered question: where does a child's imagination end and the world begin.

2021

Small found compositions — the contents of drawers, coat pockets and windowsills. A private inventory of things kept for no clear reason, photographed as if they mattered.

IV. Bio

About

Anna Bajorek is a photographer and director working between still and moving image, based in Warsaw.

Her photographic work moves between documentary observation and quiet staging — domestic interiors, landscape, and portraiture treated with the same unhurried attention. As a director, she carries the same visual language into commercial and music film work: restrained, natural light, and a preference for what is left unsaid.

Her images have been shown in group exhibitions across Poland and Central Europe, and her editorial and commercial work has been produced for clients in fashion, hospitality and culture.
